01The idea & the making

Every project
is born twice.
The second time is

A line becomes a volume. A material finds its expression. Our work is to preserve that intention through every technical decision, every joint and every finish.

Indesign and WV Design bring together fine furniture manufacturing, joinery and carpentry. WV Design carries the history of our execution. Indesign takes it forward through new collections and collaborations.
